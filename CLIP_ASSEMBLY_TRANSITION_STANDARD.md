# Clip assembly guide

Timing and audio policy: WORKFLOW_RULES.md R06–R08. This guide describes assembly practice.

Build the final sequence from approved sources using the explicit edit list and current WORKFLOW_RULES.md (which unifies the Tagpi profile). Keep story chronology, screen direction, props, eyelines, movement and wardrobe coherent. A generated source duration and its final timeline trim are separate values; a 4-second silent source can supply a 1-second evidence insert.

Use approved precise boundaries in current storyboards/manifests and verify assembled runtime. Fractional internal timing is permitted by the selected Tagpi profile; generation source tiers remain fixed. Preserve full approved lines; use room tone or an appropriate dialogue audio bridge over an insert. Hard cuts, cut-on-action and motivated visual matches are available choices. No universal fractional pause, audio overlap or fixed dialogue hold is imposed.

Inspect every join for repeated syllables, clipped speech, accidental music, identity changes and state jumps. Review the complete movie after replacing a source. Add requested licensed/approved music and branding in post, then record final review and publication authorization.
